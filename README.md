## About me

- I enjoy working at the intersection of **data science, machine learning, automation, and product design**.
- I build practical prototypes that solve real workflow problems—not just notebooks that stop at the analysis.
- My recent work explores **AI-assisted decision support**, **HR and compensation modeling**, and **document automation**.
- I care about making assumptions visible, keeping workflows understandable, and turning technical complexity into useful action.

## What I'm building

| Project | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [**CV Screener**](https://github.com/LoreJob/screener) | A candid, sector-specific CV screening skill for Claude, with ATS-oriented feedback and clear reasoning. |
| [**Compensation Optimizer**](https://github.com/LoreJob/networth-calculator) | An HR/Finance prototype that compares salary increases, benefits, welfare, tax effects, and employer cost. |
| [**Multidocument Splitter**](https://github.com/LoreJob/multidocument-splitter) | A product demo for reviewing document bundles, selecting boundaries, and tracking a deterministic processing workflow. |
